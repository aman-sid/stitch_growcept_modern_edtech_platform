## Brand & Style

This design system targets ambitious builders, tech pioneers, and high-velocity learners striving for exponential growth. The brand mood channels the kinetic momentum of the ethos: "LEARN • GROW • TRANSFORM". The interface feels like an ultra-refined, futuristic command center—combining the tactical precision of advanced developer suites with the polished luxury of contemporary high-growth accelerators.

The chosen style is **Dark Kinetic Glassmorphism**:
- Deep, light-absorbing obsidian foundations paired with semi-translucent smoke-tinted frosted glass panes.
- High-intensity energetic warm orange accents that function as beacons of progress, achievement, and actionable pathways.
- Micro-radiant gradients, refined glass refraction edges, and disciplined structural geometry that keep the interface clean, functional, and visually electric.

## Colors

The color palette is built for extreme contrast and luminous depth against pure dark environments:

- **Primary (`#FF6B00`)**: The core energetic orange driver. Applied to key calls-to-action, active progress arcs, high-priority status indicators, and focus states.
- **Tertiary (`#FF5100`)**: A fiery hyper-saturated burnt amber used for gradient terminations, active press states, glow falloffs, and interactive highlights.
- **Secondary (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure crisp white reserved for primary headlines, critical telemetry data, high-emphasis icons, and structural glass rims (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`).
- **Neutral (`#08080A`)**: Ultra-deep obsidian base. Elevated layers step upward subtly into `#0D0D11`, `#14141A`, and `#1E1E26` through layered translucent alpha overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`).

Accent functional states:
- **Success**: `#10B981` (Emerald growth)
- **Warning**: `#F59E0B` (Amber)
- **Muted Body**: `#94A3B8` (Slate tint on dark)

## Typography

Typography establishes an energetic cadence through dual sans-serif pairing:

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Contemporary, geometric, and assertive. Tight negative letter-spacing on headline levels imparts confidence and institutional velocity, reflecting exponential growth and modern tech acumen.
- **Body Text (Inter)**: Clean, utilitarian, and optimized for maximum legibility over dark glassmorphic backdrops and variable opacity panels.
- **Labels, Badges, & Meta (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Rendered in semi-bold and bold weights with uppercase tracking for taglines like "LEARN • GROW • TRANSFORM", cohort tags, and operational statistics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded on a fluid 12-column grid system designed for high-density dashboards, content feeds, and modular course architectures:

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column fluid grid, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and dynamic margins scaling up to a max container width of 1440px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px safe outer padding.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) edge gutters to maximize touch targets and horizontal screen real estate.

Spacers adhere strictly to an 8pt geometric progression (`0.25rem` to `2.5rem`), maintaining rhythmic tension between compact telemetry modules and generous narrative hero blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on opaque drop shadows; instead, it is articulated through **layered translucent surfaces, refraction borders, and energetic orange back-lighting**:

1. **Canvas Level (Ground)**: Deep solid `#08080A` with optional subtle ambient radial gradients in `#FF6B00` (opacity 4–8%, blur 120px) to ground the space in warm luminescence.
2. **Glass Base (Cards & Panels)**: `background: rgba(13, 13, 17, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Glass Float (Modals, Hovered Cards, Menus)**: `background: rgba(20, 20, 26, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`. Shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
4. **Kinetic Glow (Active & Hero Elements)**: Dynamic focus rings and interactive states cast an ambient halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 25px -4px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** (Tier 2) geometry:
- Standard buttons, input controls, chips, and sub-elements use `0.5rem` (`rounded-md` / 8px).
- Structured glass cards, containers, and modules leverage `1rem` (`rounded-lg` / 16px) to maintain a sleek, hardware-grade aesthetic.
- Hero modules and floating modal dialogs utilize `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl` / 24px).
- Badges and status pills use fully rounded continuous pill curvature (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic**: Rich gradient from `#FF6B00` to `#FF5100` with pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), bold weight, and a soft orange radiance (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -6px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.5)`). On hover, subtle upward translate (-1px) and intensified bloom.
- **Glass Secondary**: Transparent obsidian layer (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with a refined border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`), white text, and backdrop blur. Hover introduces a warm rim glow (`rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.3)`).
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Text-only or icon-accompanied button in `#94A3B8`, shifting to pure `#FFFFFF` with an orange-tinted underglow upon hover.

### Cards & Modules
- Structured on frosted glass foundations (`backdrop-blur-xl`).
- Feature top-lit pseudo-element gradients (`background: linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.0) 100%)`) forming a subtle 1px specular top highlight.
- Interactive course or growth cards trigger an ambient orange perimeter pulse on mouse hover.

### Inputs & Fields
- Dark translucent fill (`rgba(8, 8, 10, 0.7)`), 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), and text in `#FFFFFF`.
- Placeholder text in subdued slate (`#64748B`).
- Focused state transitions border to `#FF6B00` accompanied by a 3px diffused outer halo (`rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.2)`).

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill geometry with uppercase tracking (`label-sm`).
- Ecosystem tags (e.g., "TRANSFORM", "GROWTH SPRINT") style with `rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.12)` background, `#FF6B00` typography, and an energetic luminous micro-dot indicator.

### Lists & Navigation
- Flat, clean list structures framed by razor-thin dividers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Active navigation items feature an orange vertical pip or pill highlight with glass-fill surface tracking.
